Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • G-TECH

1. Vending Machine Purchases (Instant On-Site Delivery)

For consumers purchasing products at G-TECH smart automated vending machines:

  • Delivery Method: Instantaneous on-site physical delivery.
  • Delivery Time: Products are dropped into the dispensing tray within 3 to 10 seconds upon payment confirmation by UPI (PhonePe / Razorpay) or coin insertion.
  • Shipping Charges: Not applicable. There are no shipping, handling, or courier fees for purchases dispensed at physical vending machines.
  • Dispense Guarantee: Every machine is equipped with drop-detection sensors. If a product fails to drop, our automatic auto-refund is triggered as per our Refund Policy.

2. Hardware & IoT Telemetry Equipment Orders

For business clients, route operators, and franchisees purchasing commercial vending machines, reverse vending machines (RVM), or IoT telemetry boards:

  • Dispatch Origin: Dispatched from G-TECH manufacturing & assembly unit located at 32/10, sakthi vinayakar Koil street, Rajiv Gandhi nagar, nesapakkam, CHENNAI - 600078, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Dispatch Timeline: Hardware units are tested, firmware-provisioned, and dispatched within 3 to 7 business days following order confirmation.
  • Courier & Freight Partners: Shipped via registered domestic freight carriers and express surface logistics.
  • Estimated Delivery Time: Domestic deliveries across India typically take 4 to 10 business days depending on regional transit conditions.
  • Tracking: Consignment tracking details and waybill numbers are communicated via email and available in the vendor portal.

3. Cloud Software & Vendor Portal Activation (Digital Delivery)

For clients subscribing to cloud fleet telemetry, multi-tenant vendor portals, and remote machine management software:

  • Delivery Mode: Pure digital electronic delivery.
  • Turnaround Time: Software access, API credentials, and administrative accounts are activated within 24 hours of onboarding verification.
  • Delivery Channel: Login credentials and onboarding guides are delivered electronically to the registered email address.

4. Damaged or Non-Delivered Shipments

In case of physical hardware shipments arriving damaged, please notify the carrier upon delivery and contact G-TECH support within 48 hours with photographic evidence to initiate warranty repair or replacement.
