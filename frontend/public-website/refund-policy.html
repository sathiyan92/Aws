Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • G-TECH

1. Overview of Vending Services

G-TECH operates automated smart vending machines and reverse vending kiosks. Payments made via dynamic/static UPI QR codes (powered by PhonePe / Razorpay) or coins authorize instantaneous physical dispensing of items at the machine.

2. Cancellation Policy

Due to the instantaneous, automated nature of smart vending machines, once a transaction is authorized and dispensing commences, an in-progress order cannot be manually cancelled by the user. If an order fails before dispensing, the transaction is marked as aborted and no charge is completed.

3. Failed Vend & Refund Policy (Guaranteed Dispense)

Every G-TECH vending machine is equipped with optical infrared drop-detection sensors and motor current feedback to guarantee successful delivery:

  • Automatic Auto-Reversal: If a customer completes a UPI payment, but the machine detects that the item was not dropped into the tray (e.g. spiral jam, empty slot, or power failure), our cloud backend automatically triggers a refund via the payment gateway without requiring manual customer intervention.
  • Manual Refund Claims: In the rare event an automatic reversal does not trigger, customers can report the issue within 48 hours by providing:
    • Vending Machine ID (displayed on screen, e.g. VM-000001)
    • UPI Transaction Reference ID / Order ID
    • Date and time of transaction
    • Amount paid

4. Refund Processing Timelines

Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original source account (UPI ID, bank account, or debit card) used during payment.

  • UPI Payments: Typically credited within 24 to 48 hours, and at most 5 to 7 business days as per standard banking / NPCI settlement cycles.
  • Net Banking / Cards: Typically credited within 5 to 7 business days depending on the customer's bank.
