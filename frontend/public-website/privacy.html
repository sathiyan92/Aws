3. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with G-TECH, we may collect the following data:

  • Transaction & Telemetry Data: Order IDs, vending machine identifier (e.g. VM-000001), timestamp, selected slot/motor number, payment amount, and dispense outcome (drop sensor confirmation).
  • Payment Information: UPI transaction reference IDs and payment gateway status. Please note: G-TECH never collects, processes, or stores your banking passwords, debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs. Payment processing is securely handled by RBI-authorized payment aggregators (e.g., PhonePe, Razorpay).
  • Vendor & Client Account Details: Name, phone number, email address, GSTIN, and linked settlement bank account details for multi-tenant route vendor payouts.
  • Customer Support Records: Communication records when you contact our helpline or email us regarding machine assistance or refund requests.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for legitimate business and operational purposes:

  • To verify payments and trigger physical vending machine motors.
  • To automate instant customer refunds in the event of a drop sensor failure or dispense jam.
  • To settle route payouts to authorized vendor and franchisee bank accounts.
  • To monitor IoT machine health, door access, inventory levels, and power diagnostics.
  • To comply with statutory legal, tax (GST), and regulatory requirements.

5. Data Security and Storage

We employ industry-standard technical and organizational security measures:

  • All network communications between our IoT devices, web portals, and cloud servers are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Role-based access controls (RBAC) and JWT authentication protect administrative and vendor dashboards.
  • Servers are hosted in ISO 27001-certified cloud data centers with regular security patches and firewall protection.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

G-TECH does not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We disclose information only:

  • To RBI-authorized payment gateway partners (PhonePe, Razorpay) for transaction settlement.
  • When required by law, regulation, subpoena, or government authority under Indian jurisdiction.
